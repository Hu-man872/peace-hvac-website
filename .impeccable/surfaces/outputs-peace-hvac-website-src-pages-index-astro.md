# Company website

- **Scope and mode:** Persuade; five-page website covering Home, Services, Projects, About, and Contact.
- **Audience and job:** Builders, consultants, facility managers, and property owners evaluating an HVAC engineering partner for design, execution, or maintenance.
- **Action and proof:** Drive qualified quote requests through WhatsApp, phone, and email. Use supplied company-profile facts plus authentic project photography already published on PEACE's previous website; do not invent metrics, testimonials, or project names.
- **Interaction:** Clear service and sector routes, accessible mobile navigation, responsive layouts, and a quote composer that hands a structured enquiry to WhatsApp.
- **Constraints:** Fast static delivery, semantic HTML, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, and no publication of unverified claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Real installed work is the interface. Every major section reveals PEACE photography or supplied evidence immediately, while compact copy helps a visitor understand it without scrolling through an oversized introduction.

**OWN-WORLD:** An architecture-office project folio built only from PEACE's real photographs and supplied documents: bright white, deep ink navy and PEACE cyan; wide Archivo headings, neutral Source Sans 3 copy, thin rules, square-edged photographic fields, compact captions, and no generated imagery.

**STORY:** See the work first, scan the four service responsibilities and five system families, inspect more field evidence, understand delivery and safety, then discuss a project.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact utility and navigation stack; one panoramic real PEACE installation photograph; a deep-navy information block holding a maximum two-line headline, short supporting copy and two actions; the five-system rail and the next image-led section begin inside or immediately after a normal laptop viewport.

**FORM:** Real Site Folio, user-pinned by the previous PEACE website and the rejection screenshots; seed `250c809b`, assigned architecture-project-folio form, code-led because the user explicitly rejected generated concepts. Signature interaction: a restrained horizontal photograph reveal exposes the first installation image once; reduced motion shows it immediately.
