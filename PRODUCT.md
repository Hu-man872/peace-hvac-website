# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site with local assets and a browser-side WhatsApp enquiry composer.

## Users

Primary visitors are builders, consultants, facility managers, commercial property teams, and property owners evaluating an HVAC partner for a new project, upgrade, or maintenance requirement. They need to understand PEACE's actual scope quickly, inspect credible evidence, and make direct contact without navigating a long brochure.

## Product Purpose

Present PEACE HVAC Engineers as an accountable end-to-end HVAC engineering, execution, and maintenance company operating from Bengaluru across South India. Success means a qualified visitor understands the systems and services, sees evidence of real field capability, and starts a project conversation by WhatsApp, phone, or email.

## Positioning

PEACE combines requirement study, system design, supply, supervised installation, testing, handover, and maintenance under one technical team instead of presenting isolated equipment sales or generic repair services.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be comparing contractors during project planning, reviewing capability from a phone at a worksite, or searching for maintenance support for an operating building. Relevant evidence includes HVAC installation photography, the supplied client and sector record, safety practices, and regional presence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five public routes: Home, Services, Projects, About, and Contact.
- Services cover air-conditioning projects, ductwork and air distribution, copper piping, and HVAC maintenance across DX, VRF, chilled-water, ductwork, and copper-piping systems.
- The contact form validates input and prepares a structured WhatsApp message; it does not store or claim to submit data to a backend.
- Business claims must remain grounded in the supplied 2026 company profile or PEACE's previous public website.
- Do not invent project names, performance figures, testimonials, client endorsements, or certification status.
- Final public domain, current certification wording, contact details, and permissions for named client or supplier material remain publication decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the PEACE HVAC Engineers name, supplied logo, direct Bengaluru contact details, practical engineering voice, and authentic PEACE project evidence. Do not use AI-generated imagery or generated interface concepts; the website must be built from real PEACE photography, supplied company-profile material, and code-native layout.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-supplied `PEACE -COMPANY-PROFILE-2026(1).pdf_20260813_171126_0000.pdf` with company facts, services, sectors, regional presence, client record, safety policy, and certification material.
- PEACE's previous public website at `https://peacehvac.in/`, supplied by the user as a factual and visual reference.
- Authentic installation photography previously published by PEACE and stored under `public/assets/field/` with source provenance.
- User screenshots documenting rejected oversized headings, excessive section height, delayed imagery, and a text-only systems section.
- No approved testimonials, quantified outcomes, or named case-study narratives are available; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show evidence before making broad claims.
2. Let a visitor understand scope and reach within one normal viewport.
3. Keep project contact direct, visible, and low-friction.
4. Distinguish authentic project evidence from explanatory or editorial visuals.
5. Prefer concise technical language over generic corporate copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must remain responsive and keyboard accessible, with semantic landmarks, labelled controls, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, accessible validation, descriptive alternative text, and a mobile navigation model that isolates background content while open.
